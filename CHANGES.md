# Fact-check changes

Sources: Bulbapedia (bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net, page wikitext via the MediaWiki API), LeafGreen values used wherever FireRed and LeafGreen differ. Checked 2026-09-28.

## Fixed

- Game Corner Abra: said "180 coins", now "120 coins" (180 is FireRed). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celadon_Game_Corner
- Game Corner Clefairy: said "500 coins", now "750 coins" (500 is FireRed). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celadon_Game_Corner
- Game Corner Dratini: said "2,800 coins", now "4,600 coins" (2,800 is FireRed). Pinsir 2,500 and Porygon 6,500 were already right. https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Celadon_Game_Corner
- Power Plant info: said "Great spot for Electabuzz and Magnemite", now "Great spot for Magnemite and Pikachu. Electabuzz lives here in FireRed only". https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kanto_Power_Plant
- Quest 21 steps: said "See 60 Kanto Pokémon", now "Enter the Hall of Fame and visit One Island" + "Catch (own) 60 Kanto species" (Oak needs 60 owned, not seen). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/National_Pok%C3%A9dex
- Quest 21 reward: said "National Dex, Sevii 4 to 7 open", now "National Pokédex. Celio's gem hunt opens". The Rainbow Pass (islands 4 to 7) comes from Celio after you hand him the Ruby. The Ruby Path only opens after the National Dex. https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rainbow_Pass , https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mt._Ember
- Quest 22 steps: said Waterfall, Warehouse, Ruby + Sapphire, Celio. Now in game order: Ruby in Ruby Path, Celio gives the Rainbow Pass; Icefall Cave (HM07 Waterfall, help Lorelei); the Sapphire is stolen in Dotted Hole; clear the Rocket Warehouse, get the Sapphire back, bring it to Celio. Reward now also lists the Rainbow Pass. https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen/Part_18 , https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen/Part_19 , https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sapphire_(item)
- Quest 23 steps: said "Surf north of Cerulean to Cerulean Cave", now "Finish the Ruby and Sapphire quest first, or the guard blocks the door" + "Surf from Route 24 down to the cave by Cerulean City". In FRLG the cave opens once Celio's Network Machine is fixed. https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cerulean_Cave , https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen/Part_20
- Cerulean Cave info: said "Postgame only. Surf across the pond on Route 24", now "Postgame. Opens once Celio's machine is fixed (quest 22)" + "Surf down from Route 24 to reach it". https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cerulean_Cave
- Quest 6 step: said "Cross Nugget Bridge north and beat your rival", now "Beat your rival at the south end of Nugget Bridge, then cross it" (the rival fight comes first). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen/Part_5
- Quest 13 and 14 titles: said "Badge 5: Sabrina" and "Badge 6: Koga", now "Marsh Badge: Sabrina" and "Soul Badge: Koga". The Badge Case counts Soul (Koga) as #5 and Marsh (Sabrina) as #6, and either gym can be done first, so badge names avoid the clash. `grants` unchanged (b6 Marsh, b5 Soul). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen/Part_10 , https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen/Part_11
- Roaming beast: said "After the National Dex", now "After you bring the Sapphire to Celio". Starter to beast pairing was right. https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Roaming_Pok%C3%A9mon
- Three Island info: said "Wild Johto Pokémon start showing up", now "Wild Johto Pokémon only show up after the National Dex". Berry Forest and Bond Bridge have only Kanto species. https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Berry_Forest , https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bond_Bridge , https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen/Part_18
- Celadon info: said "Department Store: TMs, evolution stones, drinks for the gate guards", now says the guards want the Tea, not the rooftop drinks. https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen/Part_9
- Two Island info: said "Cape Brink: Starter's grandma teaches its ultimate move", now "a tutor teaches your fully evolved starter its ultimate move (needs max friendship)". https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cape_Brink
- Six Island info: said "Dotted Hole: the Sapphire", now adds "(a thief takes it to Five Island)". https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sapphire_(item)
- Seven Island info: said "Tanoby Ruins: catch Unown", now "catch Unown after solving Tanoby Key". https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Tanoby_Ruins
- HM05 Flash item: said "Oak's aide, Route 2, after 10 caught" with spot "forest", now "Oak's aide, Route 2 east gate, after 10 caught (needs Cut)" with spot "digletts" (the gate is at the Diglett's Cave end of Route 2, not by the forest). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kanto_Route_2 , https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough:Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen/Part_7
- Pallet catch: said "Route 21 (Surf): Tentacool; grass: Tangela, Pidgey, Rattata", now "Route 21: Tangela in the grass, Tentacool on the water" (Tangela is the only grass Pokémon in FRLG). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kanto_Route_21
- Cerulean catch: said "Goldeen and Poliwag (Good Rod)", now "Krabby and Horsea (Good Rod)" (LeafGreen city water table). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Cerulean_City
- Seafoam catch: said "Seel, Slowpoke, Zubat, Golbat, Psyduck", now "Seel, Dewgong, Slowpoke, Slowbro, Zubat, Golbat" (Psyduck is FireRed only here). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Seafoam_Islands
- Cinnabar catch: said "Grimer, Koffing, Ponyta, Vulpix, Rattata, Ditto", now "Grimer, Muk, Koffing, Vulpix, Rattata, Raticate, Ditto" (no Ponyta in the LeafGreen mansion). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mansion_(Kanto)
- Victory Road catch: said "... Geodude, Graveler, Onix ...", now drops Graveler (not wild there in FRLG) and adds Sandslash (LeafGreen). https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kanto_Victory_Road
